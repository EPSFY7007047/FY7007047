Abstract

Active Learning (Angluin, 1988; Cohn et al., 1994) is a paradigm where a concept is learnt by an intelligent machine through the interaction of an oracle with the machine system over a sequence of learning epochs. A distinctive component in an active system is the querying strategy used to sample candidate training instances from a pool and evaluate them for informativeness. Various strategies have been proposed including Query by Committee, Monte Carlo estimation, uncertainty sampling and version space reduction.

Introduction

Galaxy formation describes the process through which galaxies are created and evolve over time. Following the Big Bang, small density fluctuations in matter gradually grew under the influence of gravity. These structures accumulated gas and dark matter, eventually leading to the formation of stars, galaxies and large-scale cosmic structures. Modern observations and simulations continue to improve our understanding of galaxy evolution.

Analysis and Discussion

Current theories suggest that galaxies form within massive dark matter halos. Over billions of years, mergers between galaxies and the continuous accretion of gas influence their size, shape and structure. Observations from powerful telescopes such as the James Webb Space Telescope provide valuable insights into galaxy formation and the conditions present in the early universe.
